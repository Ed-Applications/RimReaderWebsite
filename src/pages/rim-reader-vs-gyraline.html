What that means in your driveway

Keep your case on

The bracket fits phones 67 to 91 mm wide in their case. Nothing to take apart before you start.

Hands on the bracket, not the screen

Hold the bracket level and still, and the phone takes the reading and beeps.

Mistakes are caught at the wheel

Every wheel is read twice. If the bracket didn’t go back on the same spot, the app asks for that wheel again straight away.

Know your phone is up to it

Calibration grades your phone Great, Good or Not suitable before you measure a car, so you aren’t left guessing.

An honest rating on every result

Each result is rated High, Medium or Low, with the reasons. If a lap doesn’t add up, the app shows no numbers rather than wrong ones.

Told which way to turn

After a lap, the app tells you which way to turn each front tire, so you know where to start with the wrenches.
