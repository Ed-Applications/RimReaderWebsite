About us

Made by a repair shop, for the driveway

Rim Reader is made by Middle TN Auto Repair LLC, an auto repair business in Middle Tennessee. We build the app, design the bracket, and sell it ready-printed.

Why we built it

Toe is the adjustment that wears tires fastest when it is off, and the one most people can set themselves with a wrench. Checking it has meant a trip to a shop with an alignment rack, or an afternoon with strings and a tape.

A phone already carries a gyroscope that can measure a fraction of a degree. Rim Reader holds it against each wheel the same way every time, so you can check your alignment in your own driveway, adjust, and check again.

What we stand by

  • Honest numbers. Every result carries a confidence rating, and a lap that doesn’t add up shows no numbers rather than wrong ones. Our accuracy page shows how closely readings repeat.
  • Your data stays yours. No account, and your readings stay on your phone. Privacy policy.
  • Open bracket files. The print files are free, and the app works with a bracket you make yourself.
  • Know when to see a shop. Rim Reader gives estimates, not a professional alignment. After suspension or steering work, and before highway driving or carrying heavy loads, have a qualified shop check it.
