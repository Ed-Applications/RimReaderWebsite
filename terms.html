1. What Rim Reader is. Rim Reader is a do-it-yourself measuring aid. It estimates wheel alignment angles from your phone's sensors and a bracket held against the wheel. Its readings can be wrong because of the phone, the bracket or how it was printed, where and how it is placed, the floor, the vehicle, or errors in the app. It is not professional alignment equipment, and it does not check whether the steering, suspension or tires are safe. In these terms, "Rim Reader" means the app, the bracket design, and all readings, instructions and reports, including pictures and files you save or share.

2. Safety rules. Work on firm, level ground with the engine off, the parking brake on and the wheels chocked. Never get under, or reach under, a vehicle held up only by a jack; use rated jack stands. Before adjusting, inspect the tie-rod ends, ball joints, bushings and tires, and do not adjust or reuse worn, bent or damaged parts. Follow the vehicle maker's procedure and specifications. Tighten jam nuts, lug nuts and other fasteners to the maker's torque. Center the steering wheel and check that the steering moves freely from lock to lock. Remove the phone and the bracket before moving the vehicle. Never use the app while driving. Make your first test drive slowly, in a safe, empty area; if the vehicle pulls, wanders, vibrates or makes noise, stop and have it inspected. Have a qualified shop check the alignment before highway driving or carrying heavy loads.
